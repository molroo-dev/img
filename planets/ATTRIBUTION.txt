Solar System body texture maps
==============================

The Sun, Mercury, Venus, the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune
textures are from Solar System Scope (INOVE), downloaded from:

https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

The maps are distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International license (CC BY 4.0):

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The files in this directory are the original 2048x1024 JPEG downloads, renamed
for stable application URLs. See docs/planet-texture-sources.md in the source
repository for per-file provenance and checksums.

earth-cloud_combined_2048.jpg is NASA's Blue Marble cloud composite, downloaded
unchanged from NASA Visible Earth:

https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_2048.jpg

Credit: NASA Visible Earth.

earth-blue-marble-4096.jpg and earth-night-4096.jpg are unmodified 4096x2048
EPSG:4326 exports of the NASA GIBS layers BlueMarble_NextGeneration and
VIIRS_CityLights_2012, requested from:

https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg4326/best/wms.cgi

Credit: NASA Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth
Science Data and Information System (ESDIS).
